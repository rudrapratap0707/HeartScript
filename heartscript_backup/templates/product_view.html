Why HeartScript Exists

In an era of instant messages and cold digital fonts, the warmth of a human touch has become a rare luxury. HeartScript was born from a simple realization: some emotions are too deep to be typed on a glass screen. They deserve the scratch of a nib on premium paper, the slight scent of ink, and the unique curves of a hand that cares.

Every piece we create is more than just a gift; it is a time capsule. Whether it's a love letter that will be read fifty years from now or a poem that heals a broken heart today, our artists pour their soul into every stroke.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about customizing your piece.

How do I send my custom message?
After completing checkout or via WhatsApp, you can directly share your text, letter, or poem with our customization desk.
How long does it take to create and deliver?
Since each piece is hand-scribed from scratch, creation takes 1-2 business days, followed by 3-5 days for express shipping across India.
Is the ink permanent and archival?
Yes, we use waterproof, fade-resistant archival ink on heavyweight cotton-blend paper designed to last for generations.
